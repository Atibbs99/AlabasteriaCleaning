Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who We Are

Alabasteria Cleaning Services, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates alabasteriacleaning.com. We are a San Diego–based commercial and residential cleaning company committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the personal information you share with us.

Information We Collect

We only collect information needed to respond to your inquiries, schedule walkthroughs, and improve our website:

  • Quote & Walkthrough Forms: When you request a quote or schedule a walkthrough, we collect the details you provide—such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, service address, preferred schedule, and cleaning notes.
  • Direct Communications: If you call, text, or email us directly, we retain the information you share so we can follow up and provide service.
  • Website Usage & Analytics Data: We use cookies and tracking scripts to understand how visitors find and use our site (such as pages visited, browser type, and general location).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote requests, schedule walkthroughs, and deliver cleaning services.
  • To communicate with you regarding scheduling, updates, or customer support.
  • To monitor website performance, prevent spam, and improve our services.

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on trusted third-party tools to operate our website and handle inquiries:

  • Form Processing (FormSubmit): Walkthrough and contact form submissions are securely routed to our business email so we can respond promptly.
  • Analytics & Tag Management (Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager): Used to measure site traffic and understand how visitors interact with our pages.
  • Visitor Insights (Leadsy.ai): Used to help us understand business interest in our commercial cleaning services.

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. You can limit cookies or tracking at any time through your browser settings.

Mobile & Text Message Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Any text message consent you provide is used solely to communicate with you about your cleaning inquiries or services.

Data Retention & Your Rights

We keep inquiry and client contact information only as long as necessary to provide services, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations. You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us below.
