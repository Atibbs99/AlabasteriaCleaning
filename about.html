Meet our founder

Angel Tibbs Founder & Director

A Family, a Dream and an Immense Amount of Effort

On the 9th of February 2024, I set up Alabasteria Cleaning Services. At this point, I was looking to begin the process of applying to Nursing School. Of course I didn’t fully appreciate the obstacles I would encounter in running my own business. However, I did understand that I was doing this for my family.

Like many, our household grew up not having a lot to live off. Rice and Beans were often the limit. My mother and my sisters put so much effort into feeding and caring for each other, which I will never forget. Having grown up as a 1st Generation University student, I have encountered difficulties throughout my life. But the desire to better those around me has driven me further than anything else.

As mentioned previously, my mother has worked within the cleaning sector for in excess of 10 years. I have looked up to her from a young age. Naturally, my family can provide the necessary labour and tenacity required, so where can I add value? With my skill set, I can help run and grow the operation from a technical perspective. So off we went.

We have had some struggles along the way. After around 6 months, we secured our first client. There have been days where I believe we have asked ourselves “is this going to work?”. Although we continue to come across issues, we persevere, lean on each other and remember our driving force.

Ultimately, I hope to enable my family to improve their standard of living, and ideally purchase a house. This goal is still some time away, but we continue to learn about and capitalize on any new opportunities.

However, I am most fulfilled by watching my family in action. From the effort they put into the jobs, to the care taken in their working environment, I frequently find myself thinking that I should be their customer!

So if you book a job with us, you are allowing a family to chase their dream. In doing so, we strive to deliver the best possible product for the cost you pay.

For the chance to improve others’ working environments, we are eternally grateful.

Angel Tibbs
Founder and Director, Alabasteria Cleaning Services
